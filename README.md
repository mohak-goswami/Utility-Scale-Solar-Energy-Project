# ☀️ Utility-Scale Solar Energy Project

## 3.0 MWp Utility-Scale Solar PV Power Plant

A detailed project-development and techno-economic study for a **3.0 MWp utility-scale solar photovoltaic (PV) power plant** proposed in **Santuri, Purulia District, West Bengal, India**.

This project presents an end-to-end framework for planning, engineering, financing, implementing, and operating a utility-scale renewable energy project. The report combines **solar resource assessment, PV system design, electrical infrastructure, capital expenditure, operating expenditure, debt financing, revenue modelling, taxation, grid evacuation, and long-term financial projections** into a single project architecture.

## 🔧 Technical Architecture

The proposed plant uses **700 Wp N-Type TOPCon bifacial solar modules** arranged into multiple PV strings and connected to a central inverter system.

The principal electrical energy flow is:

**PV Array → Central Inverter → Step-Up Transformer → 33 kV Switchgear → Grid Evacuation → WBSEDCL Substation**

The system incorporates:

* 4,290 deployed PV modules
* N-Type TOPCon bifacial technology
* 1,500 V DC architecture
* 2.5 MVA central inverter
* 0.615 kV / 33 kV step-up transformer
* 33 kV vacuum circuit breaker panel
* Numerical electrical protection systems
* Solar DC and AC cabling
* Earthing and lightning protection
* SCADA and weather monitoring infrastructure
* Dedicated grid evacuation infrastructure

## 📍 Site & Solar Resource Assessment

The project site is located in **Santuri, Purulia District, West Bengal**, with the study incorporating geographic, irradiance, temperature, and performance assumptions for utility-scale generation.

Key design parameters include:

* Latitude: **23.52° N**
* Longitude: **86.83° E**
* Global Horizontal Irradiance: **4.85 kWh/m²/day**
* Direct Normal Irradiance: **4.20 kWh/m²/day**
* Average Ambient Temperature: **26.8°C**
* Design Performance Ratio: **79.50%**

The site layout allocates approximately 11 acres across the PV array field, inverter and transformer yard, control/SCADA infrastructure, internal roads, drainage, and required setbacks.

### Financing Structure

The proposed financing structure consists of:

**30% Promoter Equity + 70% Project Debt**

The model assumes:

* Promoter contribution: **₹3.03 Crore**
* Term loan: **₹7.07 Crore**
* Interest rate: **8.50% p.a.**
* Repayment horizon: **7 years**

## 📊 25-Year Financial Model

The project includes a year-by-year operational and financial framework covering the full 25-year PPA period.

The financial model considers:

* Annual electricity generation
* PV degradation
* Electricity revenue
* Plant operating expenditure
* Debt servicing
* Corporate taxation
* Director compensation
* Post-debt cash generation
* Corporate treasury allocation
* Long-term investment accumulation
* Terminal liquidation

The initial operating period focuses on **debt servicing and plant operations**, while the post-debt period models the project as an unleveraged renewable-energy cash-generating asset.

## 📈 Corporate Treasury Strategy

An additional financial layer models the deployment of post-debt operational surplus into corporate treasury investments.

From Year 8 onward, the model assumes annual treasury allocations over the remaining project life, with the study projecting long-term portfolio accumulation under an assumed investment return.

This creates a second financial component alongside the underlying solar-generation asset:

**Solar Plant Cash Flow → Debt Repayment → Operating Surplus → Treasury Investment → Long-Term Asset Accumulation**

## ⚡ Grid Integration

The proposed electrical infrastructure is designed around a **33 kV grid-interconnection architecture**.

The export chain consists of:

```text
Solar PV Array
      ↓
Central Inverter
      ↓
0.615 kV AC
      ↓
2.5 MVA Step-Up Transformer
      ↓
33 kV Switchgear
      ↓
33 kV Evacuation Line
      ↓
WBSEDCL Substation
```

The project therefore considers not only PV generation but also the electrical infrastructure required to deliver generated energy to the grid.

## 🎯 Purpose of the Project

This project is intended as a **comprehensive academic and professional-style project-development model** demonstrating how a utility-scale solar power project can be evaluated from multiple perspectives rather than focusing solely on PV system sizing.

It brings together:

**Renewable Energy + Electrical Engineering + Project Finance + Corporate Finance + Infrastructure Planning + Grid Integration + Long-Term Financial Modelling**

The project can therefore serve as a reference framework for understanding the relationship between **technical plant design and commercial project viability**.
