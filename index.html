Executive Summary & System Architecture

1.1 Project Concept

The proposed project involves the establishment of a 3.02 MWp DC utility-scale solar photovoltaic power plant with full 3.0 MVA AC grid evacuation in Santuri, Purulia district, West Bengal.

The plant is structured as a Special Purpose Vehicle under a Private Limited company framework to ensure corporate tax optimization under Section 115BAA, limited liability protection, and complete bankability for long-term project financing.

The primary commercial objective is the long-term generation and sale of 100 percent green electricity to the West Bengal State Electricity Distribution Company Limited under a 25-year Power Purchase Agreement at the official High Voltage benchmark tariff of ₹3.89 per kWh - modeled conservatively at a ₹3.80 per kWh baseline for bank debt stress-testing.

The venture acts as an asset-backed, debt-leveraged wealth compounding vehicle, clearing all bank liability in 6.3 to 6.5 years and channeling post-debt operational surplus into high-yield corporate mutual fund treasury accounts.
